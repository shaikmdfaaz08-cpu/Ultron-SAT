How to Use ULTRON SAT

Follow these steps to analyze an image and explore a possible oil-spill movement scenario.

  • 1. Upload an image. Select or drag in a satellite image using the Upload and Analyze panel. For the most meaningful results, use imagery compatible with the model's training data.
  • 2. Run the AI model. If the AI result has not appeared, click Run AI model and wait for the analysis to finish. If you have already run it, you do not need to run it again unless you upload another image or want to change the analysis settings.
  • 3. Enter the location. In the Where in the World panel, enter the image's correct approximate slick latitude and longitude. The demo image has no embedded geographic coordinates, so its default location is only an example.
  • 4. Set the weather and current conditions. Enter the best available local wind speed, wind direction, current speed and current direction in the 48 h Drift Ensemble panel. Use a reliable weather or marine forecast for the selected location and time. Wind direction means the direction the wind comes FROM; current direction means the direction the water flows TOWARD.
  • 5. Review the approximate movement. Explore the 6, 12, 24 and 48-hour drift markers, the estimated flagged area and the map. These are simplified model estimates, not a confirmed spill boundary or a reliable operational forecast.
  • 6. Explore the other features. View the simulated satellite telemetry, or paste Tinkercad Serial Monitor lines and press Replay.

Important: ULTRON SAT displays AI predictions of potential oil slicks, not confirmed oil. The current drift controls use values entered by the user; the dashboard does not automatically retrieve a live weather forecast. Results depend on image quality, model suitability, location accuracy and the conditions entered.

Limitations

  • Highlighted pixels are model predictions, not confirmed oil. Low wind, algae, rain cells and ship wakes can look similar.
  • The U-Net was trained on a limited dataset imagery. Its performance in UAE waters and the wider Arabian Gulf has not yet been independently validated.
  • The baseline threshold is a brightness rule, not AI. Area depends on the km per pixel you enter.
  • The drift model ignores weathering, spreading, tides, waves and shorelines. Not for response decisions.
